CUTickets

A verified student marketplace for event-ticket interest at Columbia & Barnard. Trustworthy, collegiate, modern — a clean board, not a sketchy resale site. Below: the system, then the five key screens.

Principles

  • ◆Two semantic colors. Green = supply (selling), amber = demand (buying). Used consistently everywhere.
  • ◆Trust on the surface. Verified badges, school year, reputation, account age shown next to every listing.
  • ◆Asking ≠ confirmed. Active asks shown separately from confirmed sales so one $100 troll can't define the market.
  • ◆Off-platform honesty. A persistent, calm warning: payment/transfer happen on Posh/Partiful at your own risk.
